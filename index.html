Guandan 掼蛋

Set player names, then deal two decks among four players. Partners sit opposite each other: Players 1 & 3 versus Players 2 & 4.

This standalone edition supports the core hot-seat game flow and common Guandan combinations: single, pair, triple, triple+pair, 5-card straight, 3 consecutive pairs, 2 consecutive triples, four-or-more-card bombs, straight flush, and four-joker bomb.

Two decks are used and shuffled with the browser's cryptographically secure random generator. Jokers are highest. The ♥ card of the current level rank is a wildcard (逢人配) for supported combinations. Bombs beat ordinary combinations; straight flush beats normal bombs below six cards; larger bombs outrank smaller bombs. The current level rank is treated as a high rank for ordinary comparisons.

Game Guide

Teams: Players 1 & 3 are Team A. Players 2 & 4 are Team B.

Goal: Empty your hand. A round ends when three players have gone out; the remaining player is fourth.

Tricks: The leader plays a combination. Other players either play the same type with a higher value, use a bomb, or pass. After everyone else passes, the last player who played starts a new trick.

Play confirmation: After selecting cards and pressing Play Selected, the game shows the proposed cards and combination. Nothing is played until Confirm Play is pressed.

Pass confirmation: Pass also requires confirmation before the turn changes.

Hand privacy: Opponents' remaining card counts stay hidden until they have 10 or fewer cards.

Hand ordering: Use Reorder Cards and the arrow buttons to arrange cards manually. Sort Hand restores automatic rank sorting.

Finished-player lead: if a player has already finished and everyone passes on that player's winning play, that player's teammate leads the next trick when still active.

Supported combinations: singles, pairs, triples, triple + pair, 5-card straights, three consecutive pairs, two consecutive triples, bombs of 4+ same-rank cards, 5-card straight flushes, and the four-joker bomb.

Round scoring used in this edition: if partners finish 1st & 2nd, +3 level points; 1st & 3rd, +2; 1st & 4th, +1. The winning team advances that many levels. Score counter also records these points.

Tribute and opening lead: From the second hand onward, the opening lead is determined by the previous result. After a 1st+2nd finish, 4th place tributes 1st and 3rd place tributes 2nd; the player who paid the higher-ranked tribute leads the next hand. If those two tribute cards are equal in rank, the game randomly chooses which of the two tribute payers leads. After a 1st+3rd finish, only 4th place tributes 1st, and that tribute payer leads. After a 1st+4th finish, there is no tribute and the previous 1st-place player leads. The first hand of a new game starts with a random player. Tribute cards are transferred automatically, and the recipient returns a card ranked 10 or below when available. Each exchange is handled privately on the pass-and-play screen.

Level cards: the round's level rank is the higher/current leading team's level. Level cards rank above Ace for ordinary same-type comparisons. The ♥ card matching the current level rank is a wildcard (逢人配) and may substitute for any ordinary suited card in supported combinations; it cannot become a joker. Regional Guandan rules vary; this edition uses the tribute/return-tribute convention described above.
